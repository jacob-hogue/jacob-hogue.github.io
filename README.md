## Selected work

| Project | What it is | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **Airspace obstruction calculator** | Shows whether a planned drone flight or installation crosses FAA approach, departure, or security surfaces at military air bases and airports. Built with the ArcGIS Maps SDK for JavaScript and deployed to AirHub Marketplace | Go/no-go decisions 75% faster for 50 senior military staff |
| **Romulus, MI airspace and ground risk analysis** | Spatial risk assessment I led for the city | 34 FAA ceiling grids elevated and 13.92 square miles added to the city's drone flight area |
| **Drone Operations Assessment** | A 35-question Survey123 assessment that places a city or organization on a 6-phase UAS integration framework. A Python pipeline writes each client a 25-page report on its strengths and gaps | Report time cut by about half, and shorter pre-sales cycles |
| **Cesium drone flight simulator** | Browser-based 3D simulator that plays drone routes against terrain, airspace, and obstruction models | For the MHA Nation medical delivery pilot, a 30-mile corridor at 100 ft that cut courier time by 60% |
| **Rochester work order system** | Service requests, supervisor routing, crew dispatch, and asset history on SQL Server and ArcGIS, synced with Beehive Asset Management | 100+ city staff trained, from the front desk to the DPW director |
| **[Datum](https://datum-gis.com/)** | Public directory of government ArcGIS servers, kept current by a weekly Python status check | 9,242 servers from 5,459 federal, state, county, and city organizations |

Case studies for each project are on the [portfolio site](https://jacob-hogue.github.io/#projects).
